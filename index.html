About the studio

Named after the best breakfast in the world.

Kaya Toast Games is an independent studio in Singapore, founded by Ivan. We make small, polished pixel-art games with a lot of heart and a little mischief, and we build them in the open with our players.

Open about AIIvan designs and directs; AI tools help draw, code and compose. We say so plainly, and every asset is reviewed against the game's own art rules.
Fair playHonest hitboxes, readable spells, and rewards you earn by playing.
