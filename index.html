Inferno: Nine Circles

Sixty-nine wizards, one thread back to the sun.

In development · playable now · Steam release planned

A knockback-first battle royale for 69 wizards, set in the Greek underworld and drawn in hand-made 2.5D pixel art. Every round is a funeral game on Hecate's island while the river of fire, Phlegethon, rises. Knock your rivals into the lava; the last lamp burning wins.

Matches last about six minutes. Every spell is telegraphed, every hitbox is honest, and the same seed always plays the same match.

  • 12 playable wizards, including Greek-myth lineages: Cyclops-kin, Gorgon-born, Dryad, Satyr and Empusa
  • Hecate's island: 15 named places, from the Asphodel Meadows to the House of Hades

  • Illuminated-manuscript menus and a hand-lit night-time world

About the studio

Kaya Toast Games is an independent game studio in Singapore, founded by Ivan. We build small, polished pixel-art games with a lot of heart and a little mischief, named after the best breakfast in the world.

We are starting with Inferno: Nine Circles, built in Godot, and growing it in the open with our community on itch.io and Discord.
